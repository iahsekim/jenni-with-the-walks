Meet your walker

Hi, I'm Jenni.

I lived in Germantown for over five years with my dog, Sedona, and I always wished there was a local I could trust to walk her while I was at work, pick her up from daycare when I was running late, or pop in for a quick potty break. That person didn't exist, so I decided to become her.

After 15 years in corporate, I was ready for a change. It felt like the right time to take a break and build the business I'd been imagining.

Now I'm proud to serve my Germantown neighbors with short walks, long walks, and dog transport. I have two rescue dogs of my own, Sedona and Tofu, and they are my whole world.

I get it. Your dog isn't a pet, they're family, and I'll treat them that way.
